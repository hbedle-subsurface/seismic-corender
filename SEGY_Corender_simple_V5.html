SEG-Y Co-render

Load three attribute volumes on the same grid and co-render them as one colour image: direct RGB, ternary (LAB) or HSV blending, as in the Ternary Colormap Tool. Everything runs on this computer; nothing is uploaded.

1 · Files

SEG-Y files. Add as many as you like, then pick three below. All three must be on the same grid.

    Choose three or more files.

    2 · Blend

    Gamma, brightness from magnitude and base lightness are used in Ternary mode only (as in the ternary tool). Choose Ternary as the blend mode to use them.
