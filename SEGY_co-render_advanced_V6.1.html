SEG-Y Co-render

Co-render attribute volumes on the same grid as one colour image: three volumes by direct RGB, ternary (LAB), HSV or a colour map each, or one volume over another (e.g. an attribute in colour over amplitude in grey) — as in the Ternary Colormap Tool. Drag the histogram lines to set each range. Everything runs on this computer; nothing is uploaded.

1 · Files

SEG-Y files. Add as many as you like, then pick which drives each colour below. They must be on the same grid.

    Choose three or more files.

    2 · Blend

    Transparent parts show the background in the 2D views (checked pattern) and let you see through to the panels behind in 3D.

    Drag to rotate · right-drag or Shift-drag to move · scroll to zoom · double-click to reset
    Gamma, brightness from magnitude and base lightness are used in Ternary mode only (as in the ternary tool). Choose Ternary as the blend mode to use them.
