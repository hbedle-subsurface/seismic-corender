Seismic Co-render Tools

Co-render three seismic attribute volumes (SEG-Y) as one colour image, with inline, crossline and time-slice views, using direct RGB, ternary (LAB) or HSV blending.

Simple

Start here — the essentials, quick to learn.

  • Direct RGB, ternary (LAB) and HSV blending
  • Corner-colour presets, including colour-blind-safe sets
  • Clip %, weight and invert for each volume
  • Colour key, and save the view as PNG
Open the simple tool

Advanced

Everything in Simple, plus finer control.

  • A histogram per volume — drag the lines to set the range
  • A colour map per volume (52 maps), or your own .alut maps
  • Two volumes: an attribute in colour over a base in grey
  • Overlay opacity and threshold
  • 3D view: inline, crossline and time slice together — drag to rotate
  • Make part of any volume see-through
  • North arrow and scale bar (from the trace coordinates), vertical stretch
  • Zoom and hover for values in 2D, crop in 2D and 3D, and an enlarged view
Open the advanced tool

Before you start

  • Use Chrome or Edge. The tools also work offline: download the HTML file and double-click it.
  • The three volumes must be on the same grid. Check the inline and crossline byte locations with View file headers.
  • Your data stays on your computer. Files are read in your browser and never uploaded.
